# Personas (W3-P)

All 14 students, their parents, teachers, counsellors, mentor and tutors live in `data/personas/`. Every number a screen shows for a student (attempts, gaps, mastery, ALR, plans, timetables, career fits) comes from the real engines run on simulated answers, so the same gaps, hours, band and plan read identically on the student, parent, teacher, counsellor and tutor screens, and they move correctly when a demo user takes a new test.

```
npx tsx scripts/personas/simulate.ts          regenerate data/personas/** (byte-identical on rerun)
npx tsx scripts/personas/check-stories.ts     assert every headline fact, privacy and ID resolution
npx tsx scripts/personas/simulate.ts --search report the seed variant that makes each story hold
```

## How the data is made

- **Simulator** (`scripts/personas/lib.ts`, lifted from W2-0's `scripts/fixtures/build.ts`). Each student has latent traits: true mastery per subtopic, slips, blanks, confidence bias and calibration, time pressure, weekly forgetting, concept-group and question-type penalties, and how fast open gaps close while the plan runs. These can change over time; for example, Kabir's calibration improves from 7 October.
- **Timeline.** The history starts on the student's join date: 6 April 2026 for CBSE, 17 August 2026 for Cambridge and IB. It ends the day before `DEMO_NOW` (18 Nov 2026, 09:00 IST). Each student gets a starting test; chapter checks paced through the term; 7-day retention probes; Saturday weekly tests on open gaps, with spaced reviews for G6; mocks for Rohan and Diya; and a timetable every week, whose blocks are marked done or missed.
- **Engines.** The simulator calls `lib/data/derive.ts`, which uses `applyAttempt`, `diagnoseGaps`, `computeAlr`, `planFor`, `planInterventions` and `buildTimetable`, in time order after every test. Careers come from `mapCareers` and `careerGap`. Next best actions are computed live on read.
- **Randomness.** Each learner has their own generator, seeded `learnibility-2026:{learnerId}[:variant]`, so adding a persona never changes anyone else. When a story did not hold on the default seed, a variant was chosen with `--search`. The variant is recorded as `seed` in `scripts/personas/cast.ts`: Kabir v26, Rohan v10, Sara v12, Arjun v1, Kunal v2.
- **Questions.** Where `data/questions/{subjectId}/_status.json` says `validated: true`, attempts use the real bank: the student-check path is the first N mastery-bank items of each subtopic, the same rule as `components/assessment/catalog.ts`. Otherwise attempts use W2-0-style fixture items (`{subtopicId}-q9NN`), which are written to `data/fixtures/questions/{subjectId}.json` so their IDs resolve. `data/personas/_sources.json` lists which source each subject used in the last run. When more banks are validated, rerun `simulate.ts`, then `check-stories.ts`, and use `--search [lrn_id ...]` if a story drifts.
  - Real bank (18 Nov, 09:00): cambridge-g10-chemistry, cambridge-g10-english, cambridge-g10-maths, cambridge-g10-physics, cambridge-g12-accountancy, cambridge-g12-business, cambridge-g12-economics, cambridge-g8-english, cambridge-g8-maths, cambridge-g8-social-science, cbse-g10-maths, cbse-g10-science, cbse-g10-social-science, cbse-g11-accountancy, cbse-g11-business, cbse-g11-chemistry, cbse-g11-economics, cbse-g11-physics, cbse-g12-biology, cbse-g12-chemistry, cbse-g12-english, cbse-g12-geography, cbse-g12-history, cbse-g12-physics, cbse-g12-political-science, cbse-g8-english, cbse-g8-maths, cbse-g8-science, cbse-g9-maths, cbse-g9-science, ib-g11-economics, ib-g11-english, ib-g11-history, ib-g11-political-science, ib-g12-computer-science, ib-g12-english, ib-g12-maths, ib-g12-physics, ib-g9-maths, ib-g9-science.
  - Fixture items, until their banks are validated: cbse-g11-maths, ib-g9-english.
- **Hand-written content** lives in `scripts/personas/content.ts` and `scripts/personas/content/*.json`. It covers meetings, messages, tutor tracks, requests, applications and certificates. Its dates and gap references are read from each student's simulation, so a tutor's session report talks about a gap the engine actually found.
- **Profile.** Each student has about 200 of the 430 parameters. About 110 are seeded at onboarding (M1, M2 or M9, low confidence), the story's own values are medium confidence, and p8, p20 and p60 come from evidence (M14, high confidence). The rest fill in as history accrues. Budgets are integer rupees: p178 is the annual education budget, p180 the India UG ceiling and p181 the total abroad budget. All prices are dummy values.
- **Privacy.** T3 values (screened wellbeing and sleep) exist only in the student's own profile and in Heena's T3 meeting notes. Parents get a plain summary message. `check-stories.ts` fails if any T3 value or T3 note appears in a parent, teacher, tutor or mentor file, booking, session, message, task or invoice.

## Files

`data/personas/students/{learnerId}/` holds the same 20 files as the fixtures: `alr, attempts, bookings, careers, consents, documents, events, gaps, invoices, mastery, meetings, messages, plans, profile, requests, sessions, shortlist, student, tasks, timetable`. Where they have content, it also holds `applications`, `finance`, `assignments`, `tutorNotes` and `approvals`. A data-layer collection is skipped from contrib for any persona-backed learner, so this content now lives here. `adults/{parents,teachers,counsellors,mentors}.json`, `classes/{sectionId}.json` (persona + 29 background students each), `index.json` (picker: 14 students and every adult, with order, one-line story and `initials`; entries for other roles, e.g. W3-CRM's sales personas, are kept on regeneration).

`data/fixtures/students/` is no longer read for any learner, because every one of them has a persona folder. That folder stays in place for tests. W2-A's demo content for Nikhil and Ananya has moved from `data/fixtures/contrib/w2a-applications.json` into their persona files; the source is kept in `scripts/personas/content/w2a-moved.json`. W2-T's Rahul Verma content for Kabir and Rohan is copied from `w2t-tutors.json` on each run. Rahul's other, non-persona students stay in contrib.

## Story checks

`scripts/personas/stories.ts` holds each student's headline facts as assertions over the engine output. Examples: Kabir's MCI trend, his G4 and G9 flags and the ch05-st3 G3; the date Rohan's paid rule first fired, his recovery and his 3+ chapters of gaps; Vihaan's Chemistry R below 0.7; Diya's 4 mocks; Kunal's G7. `check-stories.ts` runs them on the written files. It also checks Zoya, the shared Kapoor account, the 13 parent accounts, that every ID resolves (questions, curriculum, tutors, adults, sections, universities, programmes, careers, bookings, meeting notes, homework), and privacy.
